Postcard mailer

8.5" x 5.5" · Double-sided · mail to your sphere or hand out at events

Download print-ready PDF

How to print it
  1. Go to office.fedex.com and choose Postcards.
  2. Size 5.5" x 8.5", landscape, double-sided.
  3. Paper: 100 lb matte cover or 100 lb gloss.
  4. Upload postcard.pdf (page 1 = front, page 2 = back).
  5. Quantity: start with 100. Check the preview, then order for pickup.

Mailing tip: USPS postcard rates only cover cards up to 4.25" x 6", so this size mails at the First-Class letter rate. Address it in the open area on the right of the back.

Family moving guide

8.5" x 11" · Single-sided · open houses, listing appointments, leave-behinds

How to print it

Thank-you note cards

4.25" x 5.5" · Double-sided · handwritten thank-yous to clients and referral partners

Download print-ready PDF

How to print it
  1. Choose Postcards at office.fedex.com.
  2. Size 4.25" x 5.5", portrait, double-sided.
  3. Paper: matte (important: you can write on matte with a regular pen; gloss smears).
  4. Upload notecard.pdf. Quantity: 50.
  5. Buy A2 envelopes (4.375" x 5.75") to mail them.

The back stays mostly blank so you can write your note by hand.
